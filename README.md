🛴 WASP — Sustainable Urban Mobility Business Case

WASP is an academic startup business case focused on a concept for a bio-based electric scooter designed for sustainable urban mobility.

The project was developed as an investor-style startup pitch, combining product positioning, market analysis, go-to-market strategy, operations, and financial planning into a complete business proposal.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective was to develop a startup concept and present it as a potential investment opportunity.

The project included:

* Product and company positioning
* Market analysis and market sizing
* Competitive analysis
* Marketing and sales strategy
* Operations planning
* Intellectual property considerations
* Financial projections
* Investment proposal
* Final investor-style pitch

💡 Business Concept

WASP proposes a premium electric scooter built around bio-based materials, sustainable design, and urban mobility.

The concept targets environmentally conscious urban consumers while positioning the product as a premium alternative within the electric mobility market.

📊 Financial Overview

The business case models an initial funding requirement of €300,000 to support product development, production setup, branding, initial inventory, and operational expenses.

The financial model projects:

* 200 units sold in Year 1
* 1,200 units sold in Year 5
* Revenue growth from €1.6M in Year 1 to approximately €10.8M in Year 5
* Positive projected operating income from Year 1
* A proposed 200% investor ROI over five years

These figures represent projections developed for an academic business case and should not be interpreted as actual company performance.
